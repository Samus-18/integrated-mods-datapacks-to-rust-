## What RC47 contained

RC47 aligned six lava skiff placements to their **13×12×17** origin-placed NBT hulls (after `offset x=-6, y=+1, z=-8`) and the six RC46 End sky islets to their **15×12×15** footprint (after `offset x=-7, z=-7`). ## Newly discovered cave entrance issue

The RC43 `mossy_forest_descent` and `highland_stone_descent` template features are **13×39×13** NBT models intended to begin roughly Y35 below the surface and reach the surface. Both `placed_feature` entries had three sequential y offsets `−16,−16,−3` and a final air block predicate `offset: [-5,+35,-5]`.

That after-descent probe checked the correct *world Y* but depended on a large offset +35 and sampled X/Z outside the origin-placed local template. The two underground `solid` checks also used X/Z outside the origin-placed NBT box. Older static QA did not test full probe coordinate semantics.

## Actual RC48 patch

Exactly **two placed-feature JSON files** changed, plus the version description in `pack.mcmeta`. The corresponding NBT templates and all other worldgen data remain byte-identical.

- Evaluate the former surface-air probe **immediately after `WORLD_SURFACE_WG` heightmap** as `matching_blocks minecraft:air, offset [-5,0,-5]`, *before* the −35 descent.
- Keep the same three y offsets `−16,−16,−3`, but add **x=−6,z=−6 on the final offset**, to centre the 13×13 template footprint around the original sample column.
- Inside the template's final coordinate system, the two solid-ground probes become **[1,10,1]** and **[3,15,3]**. Their *absolute world block positions* relative to the surface sampling point are unchanged: **(−5,−25,−5)** and **(−3,−20,−3)**. The surface air probe retains world point **(−5,0,−5)**.
- New block predicate offsets and each `minecraft:offset` component lie within −16..16. No relative +35 predicate remains in these two placed features.

**Important:** This is a code/geometry alignment fix, not proof that these templates generate successfully, that players can always traverse them, or that they connect into open natural caves. Their exact Minecraft/Horizon codec behavior requires a runtime test.
